Originally from Ohio, Claire attended the University of Cincinnati, College Conservatory of Music (CCM). After graduating summa cum laude with a BFA in Wig and Makeup Design, she moved to Macau to work with Cirque Du Soleil. Claire worked on three of their shows: one in China, another in Las Vegas, and finally in New York. Following her arrival to the city, she earned her cosmetology license while building wigs for a handful of award winning theatre hair designers. She joined IATSE Local 798 in 2014 and was a hair stylist on multiple Broadway shows.

Thereafter she began working in TV and film.

Claire is currently the hair department head for Law and Order, and lives with her family in Brooklyn.
